YOU NEED
- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip" says Apple M...
- macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer.
- A Bluetooth smart trainer or power meter. A heart rate strap is optional.
Nothing else to install.

INSTALL
1. Open WattDisco-mac.dmg.
2. Drag "Watt Disco" onto "Applications" in the window that opens.
3. Eject the Watt Disco disk (the arrow next to it in Finder).

FIRST START
The app is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time. Only do this if the file came from
someone you know.
1. Open Watt Disco from Applications. macOS says it can't be opened or verified: press Done (or Cancel),
   not "Move to Bin".
2. Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the line about Watt Disco and
   press "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
3. Open Watt Disco again if it does not open by itself, and confirm "Open Anyway".
4. Allow Bluetooth when macOS asks. Without it the app can't find your sensors.
   (Later: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth.)
You only do this once.

YOUR FIRST RIDE
1. Answer the questions: units, FTP, weight, max heart rate (no FTP? choose the ramp test).
2. Close Zwift, Garmin Connect, Wahoo and other apps that may hold the trainer.
3. Wake the sensors: pedal, and wet the heart rate strap.
4. Connect your devices, choose a workout, press Start workout.
Controls: move the mouse over the top or bottom of the chart. Space pauses, M mutes the sound.

AFTER THE RIDE
- "Connect to Strava" does not work in this version unless you have your own Strava API keys. Save the .fit
  file from the summary or upload screen and upload it at strava.com/upload/select.
- Your rides stay on your Mac, in your home folder: Library/Application Support/Watt Disco.

UPDATE OR REMOVE
- Update: quit Watt Disco and drag the newer version onto Applications (Replace). Rides and settings stay.
- Remove: drag Watt Disco to the Bin. To delete rides and settings too, delete the folder above.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
- "No devices": check that Bluetooth is on and allowed for Watt Disco, and that no other app has the trainer.
- Send me what you did, what you saw, and the file wattdisco.log from the folder above.
