Target mode

The trainer gets harder as the road gets steeper. You choose your power, cadence and gears, as on a real climb. Downhills feel lighter. The ride ends at the top.

Difficulty

100 % feels like the real road. Lower makes the steep parts easier on your legs and your gears. Your speed and time always use the real gradient and your weight.

No trainer with control connected, so ERG has nothing to steer.

Interval changes

When an interval starts or ends, ERG has to change the trainer's power. Step sends the new power at once, so you are on target as the interval begins (the trainer still takes about a second to get there). Ease moves it there gradually over 3 seconds, which feels softer on the legs, but each interval starts a few seconds late and those seconds count as off target. After ERG lets go at low cadence it always eases back in.
