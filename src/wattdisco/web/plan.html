Training plans

  • Sweet spot to thresholdMost FTP gain comes from time just below and at FTP, with a little VO2 max on top.
  • A bit more each weekTime in zone grows about 5–10 % a week, then a lighter week lets the body adapt.
  • Mostly easyTwo key sessions a week, endurance in between, never two hard days in a row.
  • No calendarRide the next session when you can. Targets are % FTP, so a test rescales the rest.

General guidance, not medical advice. Ease off when you're ill or very tired.
